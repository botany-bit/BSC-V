Academic Counselling — B.Sc. V Semester ({{ SESSION }})

Mentor–mentee scheme, weekly counselling slots, appointments and student support for all students presently enrolled in B.Sc. V Semester.

🎯 Objectives of the scheme

  • Provide every V Semester student a dedicated faculty mentor for the session.
  • Support study planning, backlog clearance and examination preparation.
  • Guide students on higher studies (M.Sc., CSIR-UGC NET, GATE, JAM, CUET-PG) and careers.
  • Identify academic stress early and connect students with well-being support.
  • Maintain a confidential record of mentoring interactions.

🔁 How it works — 4 steps

  1. Register online for the mentor–mentee scheme using your Roll / Enrolment number. Register now →
  2. Check your allotted mentor in the Mentor Directory after the allotment list is displayed.
  3. Meet your mentor in the weekly slot, or book an appointment for a one-to-one session.
  4. Follow the action plan agreed with your mentor; a mid-semester review is held in September–October.

📚 Academic guidance

Study plans, backlog strategy, doubt clinics, laboratory & project mentoring, semester-exam preparation.

🧭 Career & higher studies

Roadmaps for M.Sc. admissions, CSIR-UGC NET, GATE, IIT-JAM, CUET-PG, internships and research exposure.

🌱 Well-being support

Confidential listening, stress and time management, and referral to the University counselling services when needed.
