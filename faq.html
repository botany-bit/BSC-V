Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about mentoring, admission counselling, appointments and grievances for Session {{ SESSION }}.

Academic counselling (B.Sc. V Semester)

Is mentor–mentee registration compulsory? Is there any fee?

Yes, registration is compulsory for every student of B.Sc. V Semester so that a faculty mentor can be allotted to you. There is no fee for registration or for any mentoring session.

How will I know who my mentor is?

The allotment list is displayed as per the schedule (Session {{ SESSION }}: from 24 August 2026) on this portal and the Faculty notice board. You can also check the Mentor Directory for your mentor's weekly slot and room.

How often should I meet my mentor?

At least once every fortnight. The first meeting for {{ SESSION }} must be completed by 31 August 2026, followed by a mid-semester review in late September / early October.

Can I change my allotted mentor?

Mentor change is permitted only in exceptional circumstances. Submit a grievance under "Academic / Mentoring" with a valid reason; the Convener, Counselling Cell takes the final decision.

Is the discussion with my mentor confidential?

Yes. Mentoring conversations are confidential. Only a brief record of the meeting (date, broad topic, action plan) is maintained for University records.

Admission counselling (new entrants)

Can I edit my choices after locking?

No. Choices once locked cannot be modified. You may re-order or add choices any number of times before locking within the choice-filling window (18 – 26 August 2026).

What if I miss the Round-1 reporting dates?

The allotted seat is cancelled automatically and offered in the next round. You may participate in later rounds if you had not withdrawn your registration.

What is "upgradation"?

If you accept a seat but opt for upgradation, you are automatically considered for your higher preferences in the next round. If upgraded, you must accept the new seat; the previous one goes back into the pool.

Which documents must be in original?

Class X certificate, Class XII marksheet & certificate, transfer/migration certificate, character certificate, and category certificate (if claimed) must be produced in original at verification, along with one set of self-attested photocopies.

Appointments & grievances

Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Contact the Counselling Cell helpdesk or your mentor by e-mail at least one working day in advance, quoting your appointment ID (A…), and book a fresh slot on the portal.

How long does a grievance take to resolve?

The Counselling Cell responds within 3 working days. Matters needing inquiry may take longer; you will be informed of the current status by e-mail.

Whom do I contact for technical issues with this portal?

Write to the helpdesk at sci.counselling@amu.ac.in with a screenshot and your reference ID. See the Contact page for office hours.
