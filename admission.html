Admission Counselling — B.Sc. (Hons.) {{ SESSION }}

Round-wise counselling for candidates shortlisted for admission to B.Sc. programmes: registration, choice filling, seat allotment, document verification and reporting.

Counselling process — 6 steps

1

Online registration

Register on this portal within the notified window using your application details. Register →

2

Choice filling & locking

Fill subject-combination preferences in order of priority and lock them before the deadline.

3

Seat allotment

Round-wise provisional allotment is published on the portal and the notice board.

4

Document verification

Report in person with original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies.

5

Fee payment & confirmation

Pay the admission fee within the due date to confirm the allotted seat.

6

Upgradation rounds

Willing candidates are considered for higher preferences in subsequent rounds.

📄 Documents required at verification

  • Provisional allotment letter / counselling reference ID
  • Class X certificate (date of birth proof)
  • Class XII marksheet & passing certificate
  • Transfer / migration certificate (original)
  • Character certificate from last institution
  • Caste / category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS), if applicable
  • PwD certificate, if applicable
  • Four recent passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar card / valid photo ID
⚠️ Please note: Allotment is provisional and subject to verification of eligibility and documents. Candidates who fail to report within the notified dates forfeit the allotted seat, which is then offered in the next round. No separate initiation fee is charged for counselling registration.
